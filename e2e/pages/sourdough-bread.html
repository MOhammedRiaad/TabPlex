Sourdough bread at home

Sourdough bread needs only flour, water, salt and a live starter. Mix the flour and water first, rest the dough for thirty minutes, and then add the salt and the starter before the first set of folds.

Bulk fermentation

During the bulk fermentation the dough rises at room temperature for four to six hours. Give it a fold every half hour for the first two hours, so the gluten builds structure without any kneading.

Baking

Bake the loaf in a hot covered pot for twenty minutes, then remove the lid and bake until the crust is deep brown. Let the bread cool on a rack for at least an hour before slicing it.
