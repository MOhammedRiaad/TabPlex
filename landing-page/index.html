Built around how you actually work

TabPlex opens in its own tab from the toolbar icon. Three things do most of the work.

Park & Resume

A task can own a set of tabs. Start it and its tabs open in a Chrome tab group named after the task.

  1. Start a task

    Its tabs open in a tab group. New tabs you open join it automatically.

  2. Park it

    Type where you left off. The tabs close and the task remembers them.

  3. Resume

    One click on Today reopens the group and shows your note. An optional on-device summary can remind you what you were doing.

Organize loose tabs in one click

TabPlex suggests Chrome tab groups for the tabs you have open: by what you're working on, using your browser's built-in AI, or by site when AI isn't available. Rename, recolor or drop tabs in a preview, then create the groups.

  • AI runs on your computer, never in the cloud
  • Pinned tabs and existing groups are never touched
  • Undo, or save the groups to a board for later

Boards for the tabs you keep

Save tabs into folders, search them, drag them around and pull in pages from your browser history. Keep a separate board for work, home or a side project.

  • Folders with colors, tags and pins
  • Tasks in To Do, Doing and Done columns with priorities, due dates and checklists
  • Command palette (Ctrl+K) to reach everything

And the rest of the workspace

Markdown notes
Capture ideas next to your tasks. Link a note to a board, folder or tab.
Study notes from tabs
Pick open tabs and Summarize open tabs turns them into one combined note with a link to every source. TabPlex reads those pages only on sites you allow, on your device.
Focus timer
A Pomodoro timer that can start with a task and pause when you park it.
Local insights
See how often you switched context and how many tabs parking closed for you.
Sessions and bookmarks
Browse and organize your bookmarks, and save tab sessions to restore later. Sessions are only created when you ask.
Canvas
A whiteboard for sketching plans and diagrams beside your work.
Export and backup
Export a task or board as Markdown or CSV, or back up everything to a file. Imports are checked first, so a damaged file is rejected before anything changes.
Light and dark
Follows your system theme, or pick one yourself.

Your tabs stay on your device

TabPlex keeps everything in your browser. There's no account, no TabPlex server and no analytics.

Read the privacy policy
  • Your tasks, notes, boards and tabs are stored on this device only.
  • AI features use your browser's built-in model on your computer. Tab data never leaves it.
  • No site access when installed: TabPlex sees tab titles and addresses, and reads a page's text only for tabs you pick to summarize, on sites you allow.
  • Website icons come from your browser's own local cache, not a third-party service.
  • Recording the pages you open is a setting you control, and Clear All Data turns it off.
  • Export everything to a file at any time, or clear it all in Settings. The optional canvas keeps its own storage until you uninstall.

Questions

Is TabPlex free?

Yes. TabPlex is free on Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

Does TabPlex send my tabs or notes to a server?

No. TabPlex has no account, no TabPlex server and no analytics. Your tasks, notes, boards and tabs are stored in your browser on this device only.

Can TabPlex read the pages I visit?

Not on its own. TabPlex has no website access when installed and never reads pages in the background; it sees tab titles and addresses. Only if you pick tabs in Summarize open tabs, and allow those sites when your browser asks, does it read the text of those pages, on your device. You can remove the access in Settings.

Do I need built-in AI to use TabPlex?

No. On-device AI is optional and runs on your computer through your browser's built-in model. Every feature still works without it.

Can I use TabPlex in Google Chrome?

TabPlex is on Microsoft Edge Add-ons now, and the Chrome Web Store listing is coming soon. Until then, Chrome users can download the latest release from GitHub and load it in Chrome.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export everything to a file at any time and import it back later. Clear All Data in Settings deletes your data from TabPlex; the optional canvas keeps its own storage until you uninstall.

Does TabPlex record the pages I open?

Only if you let it. TabPlex stores the tabs you add to a board or task. "Record every page I open" in Settings is an optional list that stays on your device; it is off for new installs. If you installed TabPlex before that setting existed, it stays on until you turn it off or use Clear All Data. Sessions are only created when you click Infer in Sessions.
