Park & Resume

A task can own a set of tabs. Start it and its tabs open in a labelled Chrome tab group. Switching to something else? Park it with a one-line "where I left off" note and the tabs close. Resume later and you're back exactly where you were.

  • Tab groups named and coloured after the task
  • New tabs join the active task automatically
  • Parked work waits for you on the Today view
  • Optional on-device summary of what you were doing
✨

Organize tabs in one click

TabPlex suggests Chrome tab groups for your loose tabs: by what you're working on with Chrome's built-in AI, or by site when AI isn't available. Rename, recolour or drop tabs in a preview, then create the groups. Changed your mind? Undo.

  • AI runs on your computer, never in the cloud
  • Pinned tabs and existing groups are never touched
  • Save the groups to Boards for later

How Park & Resume works

Three steps, no lost tabs.

  1. 1

    Start a task

    Add the tabs you're using to a task, or just start it. Its tabs open in a Chrome tab group named after the task.

  2. 2

    Park it

    Type where you left off. The tabs close, and the task remembers them and your note.

  3. 3

    Resume

    One click on Today reopens the group and shows your note. Closing the group by hand parks it safely too.

Frequently asked questions

The short answers about price, privacy and browsers.

Is TabPlex free?

Yes. TabPlex is free on Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

Does TabPlex send my tabs or notes to a server?

No. TabPlex has no account, no TabPlex server and no analytics. Your tasks, notes, boards and tabs are stored in your browser on this device only.

Can TabPlex read the pages I visit?

No. TabPlex requests no website access. It only sees tab titles and addresses, not the content of the pages.

Do I need Chrome's built-in AI to use TabPlex?

No. On-device AI is optional and runs on your computer through Chrome's built-in model. Every feature still works without it.

Can I use TabPlex in Google Chrome?

TabPlex is on Microsoft Edge Add-ons now, and the Chrome Web Store listing is coming soon. Until then, Chrome users can download the latest release from GitHub and load it in Chrome.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export everything to a file at any time, or delete all of your data in Settings.

Private by design

TabPlex keeps everything in your browser. There's no account, no TabPlex server and no analytics.

  • Your tasks, notes, boards and tabs are stored on this device only
  • AI features use Chrome's built-in model on your computer; tab data never leaves it
  • No access to the content of the websites you visit: TabPlex only sees tab titles and addresses
  • Website icons come from Chrome's own local cache, not a third-party service
  • Export everything to a file at any time, or delete it all in Settings
